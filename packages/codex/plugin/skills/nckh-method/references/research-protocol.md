# Scientific research protocol

## Question and claim

Define the target system/population, research question and claim type: descriptive,
predictive, associational, causal, explanatory or simulation. State candidate
hypotheses, rival explanations, discriminating predictions, falsifiers and
indeterminate outcomes. Literature absence does not establish novelty.

Identify the estimand before the estimator: unit, population, intervention or
exposure when applicable, comparator, outcome, time horizon and summary. Explain
which evidence could distinguish a symptom ranking from an identified cause.
For software research, separate code correctness, benchmark prediction, deployed
system behavior and generalization beyond the observed systems.

## Design and validity

Specify independent units, nesting/repetition, sampling, measurement validity,
allocation/blocking when applicable, interference, controls, exclusions and
missingness. Define data/label access, actual membership and chronological/group
split axes. Learned transforms fit on training data; threshold/model selection
uses the validation scope; test access and prior exposure are disclosed.

For forecasting, features must be available at the prediction origin. For RCA,
incident-window diagnostics may be allowed by the frozen protocol; labels/gold
answers are separate from model observations. Do not apply a universal ban on
post-incident telemetry without considering the task's decision time.

Record baselines, metrics, ties/no-answer, coverage/failure denominators, uncertainty,
multiplicity, stopping and sample/precision rationale. Choose these from the
question and actual resources, not a preferred winner. No universal split ratio,
power threshold, alpha or runtime package is imposed.

## Freeze and handoff

Use a dated protocol freeze suited to the task; preregistration is a separate
actual act with its own receipt. Disclose prior access, exploration, amendments
and changed decisions chronologically. An amendment invalidates affected
downstream results; it does not erase prior failures or turn exposed data blind.

Return the protocol and required domain/ethics/data/runtime grants. Method does
not run a benchmark or authorize collection from credentials/config presence.
Handoff intake/splits to dataset, observation mapping to telemetry, evaluation
to AIOps, analysis to statistics and execution lifecycle to cook/devops. Keep
literary/argument routes in the existing method checklist.

This owned reference draws on reviewed local hypothesis-generation and
experimental-design anatomy; it imports no upstream CLI, equipment, provider or
preregistration submission workflow.
