# RCA, anomaly and forecasting

## RCA

Declare cause unit (service/indicator/code), annotation semantics and multiple accepted
causes. Rank unique candidates with frozen k and tie groups; the core helper uses
contiguous stable-ID order. Report hit@k and first accepted reciprocal rank over issued
incidents, failed/unknown/no-answer coverage and competing explanations. Correlation,
SHAP, inferred topology and agreement with annotations do not identify cause.

## Anomaly

Freeze point/event/incident labels, event windows/tolerance, matching and duplicate
alert rule. Keep false-alert exposure and units, normal versus unknown windows and
detection delay. Precision has an alert denominator, recall a known-positive denominator;
neither is the count of time points when evaluating events. Scores require independent
calibration evidence before being called probabilities. Threshold selection uses validation.

## Forecasting

Declare target, horizon, origin, rolling/chronological policy and source numerical scale.
Inputs/covariates must be available at origin; origin precedes target. Audit actual source
publication vintage separately from a retrospective calendar-year index. Keep naive and
seasonal baselines when applicable; fixed arithmetic baselines need no invented RNG seed.
Record missing/failed horizons and aggregation with coverage. Learned preprocessing,
model selection, test access and pretrained overlap have separate leakage checks.

No claim of superiority follows from a single country series or a demonstration with
prior input access. Statistics owns dependence/uncertainty and method owns causal design.
