# Bounded reviews in software and AIOps research

## Search and screening

Freeze the question, review mode, scope, source/database coverage, date windows,
inclusion/exclusion, access constraints and extraction fields. Systematic synthesis
needs its protocol before screening; narrative/scoping searches remain labeled.
Record each exact query/filter, database/API/version, execution date/timezone,
export format/hash, actual retrieved count and unavailable access. Source ranking
and venue prestige do not establish evidence support.

Deduplicate reports using stable DOI/publication identifiers and documented title,
author/year/version rules when IDs are missing. Preserve preprint, conference,
journal, correction and retraction versions. Link multiple reports of the same
study instead of counting them as independent experiments. Ambiguous duplicates
remain reviewable; do not silently overwrite conflicting extracted findings.

Retain exclusion reasons at title/abstract and full-text stages, eligible counts,
screening responsibility and disagreements. Abstract-only evidence cannot support
unread results or complete methodological appraisal. Record empty searches as
observed search outcomes; novelty is bounded by coverage, dates, language and access.

## Study appraisal and synthesis

Extract the actual task, systems, benchmark release/suite, modalities, unit, data
and labels, split/cutoff, baseline and tuning access, metric/denominator, failures,
compute/budget and uncertainty. Distinguish study-level findings from claims in
each report. Appraisal matches the design; clinical checklists are not universal
software quality scores.

Inspect contamination and data availability, duplicated incidents/documents,
test-tuned preprocessing/thresholds, missing/failed prediction exclusions,
incomparable task or compute budgets, selected favorable workloads and omitted
negative results. Distinguish predictive performance, association, intervention
effect and proposed mechanism. Preserve counterevidence and alternative explanations.

Synthesize comparable tasks separately; state heterogeneity and incompatible
units/metrics. A weighted venue score is not a risk-of-bias or causal assessment.
Keep publication policy (venue/year/category/track/article type) task-specific.
Return a bounded evidence gap with the searches/access that support it, potential
counterexamples, what remains unknown and a feasible discriminating protocol.

Method owns design, dataset owns intake/splits, telemetry owns signals, statistics
owns inference, AIOps owns evaluation and evidence owns exact claim/source support.
Discovery does not launch an experiment or convert a review into human gold.
