# Method and argument routes

Empirical protocol: question, hypothesis, estimand/unit/population, sampling,
variables/control/confounding, measurement/data rights, train/test isolation,
analysis/uncertainty, sample/stopping rationale, preregistration/amendments when
applicable, validity threats and ethics/domain approval.

Literary argument: thesis, corpus/edition/translator/context, premises and exact
evidence, competing interpretations, counterarguments, comparison axes and scope.
An interpretation may be legitimate without being an experimental causal result.

Comparison: choose axes from the question, not from a preferred winner. Record
incomparable/missing cells and assumptions. A method plan does not create observed
results or satisfy a lecturer/domain/ethics signoff.
