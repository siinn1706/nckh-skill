# Fidelity, glossary and protected regions

Freeze before/after slots for numbers, units, denominators, negation, modality,
certainty, population, time, causal language, terminology, citations and limitations.
Preserve quotes/protected bytes exactly unless their revision is explicitly requested.
Structured deterministic comparison detects declared-slot changes; it cannot prove
the semantic extraction is complete. Human/domain review remains independent.

Task glossary: source term, target term, domain/edition/context, allowed variants,
decision owner/revision and unresolved ambiguity. Do not impose a global glossary.

VI/EN translation must preserve evidential certainty, causal scope and ambiguity.
New/stronger factual claims require claim/evidence records. A successful style pass
cannot clear an unresolved fidelity delta.
