# Vietnamese style by task

Match the requested audience/genre and user's preferences. Use concrete subjects,
verbs and examples, coherent sentence rhythm and clear paragraph ownership.
Reduce stock academic padding when it obscures meaning; do not impose a banned-word list.
Keep domain/literary vocabulary, ambiguity and register intentional.

Scientific writing preserves evidence/certainty/limits and venue-specific reporting.
Literary critique preserves edition/translation/context and interpretation boundaries.
General/marketing prose follows its own purpose; avoid forcing paper format.

Taste is advisory. Rights-cleared reference samples and actual human/native preferences
own personal acceptance. Grammar fluency cannot certify factual/domain fidelity.
