## Criteria

- The delivered artifact answers the exact brief, selected domain, audience and locale.
- Each required output exists and can be inspected at the recorded final hash.
- Facts, assumptions, uncertainty and unsupported conclusions are distinguishable.
- Every factual delta has evidence or remains pending; protected material is preserved.

- Failures, missing inputs and unavailable capabilities remain visible.
- Downstream work respects the chosen plan-only, auto or interactive boundary.

For each criterion record pass, fail, pending or justified not-applicable with a
located observation and required edit. Keep separate technical, agent, native and
human verdicts. Aggregate only the required checks for the approved scope; a
pending or failed required gate prevents acceptance. Freeze reviewer roles and
coverage before a qualification run; zero reviewed tasks is not a passing rate.
