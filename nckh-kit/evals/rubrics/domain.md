# Domain validity review proposal

Status: proposal; competent domain reviewer approval and thresholds are pending.
Record observations using [the rubric catalog](catalog.json).

## Criteria

- Sources support the precise claims, including methods, results and limitations actually read.
- Methods, comparisons, denominators and causal conclusions match the study design.
- Contradictions, uncertainty, alternative explanations and scope limits are preserved.
- Source version, correction/retraction status, date and exact locators are traceable.
- Venue rules belong to the selected year, track and article type; ranking has its own evidence.
- Literary analysis preserves edition, translator, language, quotation and textual context.
- Reported code, experiments and observations have real execution evidence of the stated scope.

Use domain-appropriate criteria selected before the run. Do not infer scientific
validity from grammar, a DOI, publication type, model confidence or a passing test.
A model reviewer is an automated critic, not human gold. Record unsupported or
inaccessible evidence as pending or fail according to the approved protocol;
freeze that decision rule before review rather than changing it after results.
