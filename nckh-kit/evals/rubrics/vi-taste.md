# Vietnamese taste review proposal

Status: proposal; native-reader approval and thresholds are pending. This is a
personal writing preference review, not an AI detector or scientific validity test.
Record observations using [the rubric catalog](catalog.json).

## Criteria

- Register and vocabulary fit the audience, genre and owner's stated preferences.
- Sentence rhythm, information order and paragraph flow are easy to follow.
- Concrete wording conveys the intended idea without stock phrases or forced emphasis.
- Terminology, names, numbers, units, negation and citations remain faithful to the source.
- Suggestions identify the exact passage and distinguish defects from subjective choices.
- Local changes preserve the writer's voice; a full rewrite has separate authorization.

Use rights-cleared Vietnamese reference samples only within their permitted uses.
The owner may judge personal taste; a domain reviewer owns scientific meaning.
Record actual edit time and preferences when observed, otherwise unknown. Separate
reference/development documents from protected holdout by document, author, topic
and claim family. Disclose reviewer exposure and failed blinding; exposed holdout
becomes development. Missing samples, reviewer or approved threshold stays pending.
