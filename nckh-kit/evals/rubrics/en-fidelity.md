# English fidelity review proposal

Status: proposal; English/domain reviewer approval and thresholds are pending.
Record observations using [the rubric catalog](catalog.json).

## Criteria

- The English text preserves the source's meaning, population, time and scope.
- Numbers, units, denominators, signs, negation, conditions and protected text are unchanged.
- Modality, certainty, causality and limitations do not become stronger or disappear.
- Domain terms and glossary choices remain consistent; ambiguity is surfaced.
- Quotes and citations retain their identities, locators and support relationship.
- Syntax, cohesion and register fit the requested genre without imposing IMRaD universally.

Review translation against the actual source and polish against its prior revision.
Locate each material difference; route new facts to evidence review. Separate language
fluency from domain fidelity and personal style. A fluent text with an unsupported
claim cannot clear fidelity. Store source texts and labels privately under cleared
rights. Freeze review coverage and acceptance criteria before evaluation; absent
source access or competent reviewers yields pending.
