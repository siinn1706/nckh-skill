# NCKH experimental skill kit

43 English instruction skills: 16 Core, 13 Engineer, 13 Marketing and one Xia
tooling skill. Task artifacts follow the requested Vietnamese, English or bilingual
locale. Same-agent execution is the default; delegation needs a concrete benefit.

This is a local experimental candidate. Native discovery, model effectiveness,
human writing quality and scientific visual acceptance remain unverified.
