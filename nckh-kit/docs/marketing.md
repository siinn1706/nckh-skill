# Marketing kit

Thirteen domains retain competitor research under market-research and identity/design
brief under brand. Marketing-plan owns strategy; Core plan/cook own execution.
Campaign and launch share content without duplicating a state engine.

Content/copy/email/social produce usable drafts. Drafting/analysis does not grant
spend, send, post, contact upload or traffic mutation. Credential availability is
not permission. [Provider bindings](../extensions/providers/marketing/contract.json)
remain unavailable until the task selects an authorized qualified integration.

Analytics needs actual numerator/denominator/time/data-quality records; experiment
owns randomization, primary metric, stopping/guardrails and bounded causal inference.
No fabricated market statistics, testimonials, scarcity, organic results or lift.
Marketing facts use shared evidence without mandatory scientific ranking.

CRO includes canonical/noindex inspection of the supplied conversion page, as
authorized by the owner on 2026-10-02. It reports located markup/header evidence,
unknown live indexing and discoverability hypotheses. SEO retains broader crawl,
indexing and search analysis. The earlier direct-development CRO failure remains
in its original corpus and receipts; this scope change does not regrade it.
