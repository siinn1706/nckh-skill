# Marketing kit

Thirteen domains retain competitor research under market-research and identity/design
brief under brand. Marketing-plan owns strategy; Core plan/cook own execution.
Campaign and launch share content without duplicating a state engine.

Content/copy/email/social produce usable drafts. Drafting/analysis does not grant
spend, send, post, contact upload or traffic mutation. Credential availability is
not permission. [Provider bindings](../extensions/providers/marketing/contract.json)
remain unavailable until the task selects an authorized qualified integration.

Analytics needs actual numerator/denominator/time/data-quality records; experiment
owns randomization, primary metric, stopping/guardrails and bounded causal inference.
No fabricated market statistics, testimonials, scarcity, organic results or lift.
Marketing facts use shared evidence without mandatory scientific ranking.

CRO diagnoses only: it ranks friction hypotheses and hands authorized page/UI edits
to nckh-frontend and copy changes to nckh-copy. A canonical or noindex conflict seen on
a conversion page is a secondary observation handed to nckh-seo, which owns crawl,
indexing and search analysis. Light polishing or translation of existing text belongs
to nckh-humanwrite; new copy or a changed angle/CTA belongs to nckh-copy. These and
other owner rules live in the [route boundaries](../core/registry/catalog/route-boundaries.json).
Earlier CRO results remain in their original corpus and receipts; this scope change
does not regrade them.

Email and social determine the recipients' jurisdiction first and apply only the
sources the [personal-use profile](../core/profiles/acceptance/personal-use.json) lists
for them. When no listed source covers the jurisdiction or rule, the legal gate stays
`pending`; instrument numbers and fines are never cited from memory.

On Windows, run Python tools as `python -X utf8` and keep Vietnamese file names and
encodings intact; see [host shell robustness](../core/workflows/execution.md#host-shell-robustness).
