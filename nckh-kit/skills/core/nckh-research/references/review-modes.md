# Review modes and reader cards

Narrative: bound the question/search and disclose selection limits.
Scoping: freeze scope, sources/queries, dates, screening/exclusion and extraction fields.
Systematic: freeze protocol, sources/query reproducibility, inclusion/exclusion,
deduplication, screening responsibilities, extraction, risk-of-bias/quality approach
and synthesis before running. Record amendments instead of rewriting the protocol.

Reader card fields: source ID/version/hash/access/rights/status/as-of, question,
method/corpus/data, observed argument/results, exact evidence locators/context,
limitations, counterevidence and unresolved checks. Abstract-only observations must
stay abstract-only; an absence in the searched set is not proof of global novelty.

Literary cards additionally identify edition/translator, original wording,
line/page reference and relevant historical/interpretive context. Eligibility/ranking
filters require their own issuer/category/year evidence and do not establish support.

Software/AIOps studies additionally distinguish reports from studies, actual
benchmark releases/modalities, independent units, split/tuning access, compute,
contamination, omitted failures and bounded evidence gaps. Use the linked
[software review protocol](software-research-review.md) when applicable.
