# Scientific intake, lineage and splits

Record source locator/version/as-of, archive/raw hash and component-specific rights.
Private or restricted inputs stay in the authorized project, outside kit packaging.
Tokenization alone does not establish anonymization or permission to share.

Canonical normalized rows include sample_id and declared fields; the sample index
binds entity/incident/group and time intervals. Validate actual membership bytes,
coverage/exclusions and overlap, rather than treating hash/count equality as proof
of disjointness. Do not reuse kit qualification author/topic splits for experiments.

Record every transformation's code/config/input/output hashes and reconciled row
counts. Missing tokens keep their types and meaning; zero is an observation.
Preserve conflicting labels with source/annotator/adjudication and access records.

Chronological/group/incident separation follows the scientific question. Training
fits transforms; validation chooses thresholds. Gold/answer access and frozen test
exposure have their own owner. A split/code/config amendment invalidates descendants;
keep earlier failures and receipts. Dataset acceptance and scientific release still
require domain/owner judgment after mechanical checks.
